Swayamsevak Foundation is committed to protecting the privacy, dignity, and confidential information of our volunteers, donors, beneficiaries, and visitors.

Official Policy Effective: January 2024 • Last Updated: 2026
Print Policy

1. Organization Details

This Privacy Policy applies to the official web portal and community operations of {{ foundation.name|default:"Swayamsevak Foundation" }} ({{ foundation.tagline_mr|default:"स्वावलंबनातून समाजसेवा" }}), located at {{ foundation.address|default:"TV Center, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, 431001" }}, Maharashtra, India.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the essential personal information required to facilitate social service operations, volunteer coordination, and donor communication:

  • Volunteer Registration: Full name, phone number, email address, residential city, skills, areas of interest, and availability.
  • Inquiry & Support Pledges: Contact person details, preferred mode of contribution, and inquiry notes.
  • Event Participation: Attendance records, event registrations, and role allocations.
  • Technical Logs: Basic server logs, IP addresses, and login attempts for platform security and audit compliance.

3. How Information is Used

All personal information collected is utilized exclusively for non-profit humanitarian and administrative purposes, including:

  • Organizing Sunday food distribution, blanket distribution, and blood donation drives.
  • Contacting registered volunteers regarding scheduled events and emergency service calls.
  • Responding to donor inquiries, public queries, and collaboration proposals.
  • Maintaining role-based administrative accountability and operational audit trails.

4. Role-Based Access & Data Confidentiality

In accordance with strict security standards:

  • Volunteers: Can access and edit only their own personal profile and view public event details. Volunteers never have access to other volunteers' private records.
  • Coordinators: Can access volunteer rosters strictly for events and activities explicitly assigned to them by administrators.
  • Administrators: Maintain full oversight for verification, account approval, and security audit logging.
  • No Commercial Exploitation: Swayamsevak Foundation never sells, rents, leases, or trades any user, volunteer, or donor data to commercial vendors, marketing agencies, or third parties.

5. Beneficiary Dignity & Media Policy

We respect the dignity, privacy, and safety of all individuals we serve, especially children in orphanages, patients in government hospitals, and elderly persons in old-age homes:

  • Photographic and video documentation is captured and published strictly with appropriate consent and for genuine accountability.
  • No sensationalized or undignified media is recorded or exhibited.
  • Artificial, stock, or fabricated images are strictly prohibited on our platform.
