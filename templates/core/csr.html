Every single week, our youth-driven volunteer network organizes Sunday nutrition drives, health checkups, blood donation camps, and elderly care visits. {% endif %}

Corporate Collaboration Modes

Flexible avenues for strategic social partnerships

  • Direct Project Sponsorship Adopt a specific initiative (e.g. 6 months of weekly Sunday nutrition drives or 1,000 blanket distributions).
  • In-Kind & Material Supply Contribute dry ration kits, school bags, notebooks, blankets, medical diagnostic kits, or gaushala fodder.
  • Employee Engagement & Volunteering Enable corporate staff to volunteer hands-on alongside our team during weekend drives.
  • Recurring Social Grants Quarterly or annual grants supporting ongoing community welfare, orphanage supplies, and youth outreach.

Institutional Transparency & Statutory Compliance Disclosure

Swayamsevak Foundation operates as a transparent, youth-driven grassroots social service organization established in January 2024. Important Statutory Notice: Statutory registrations such as Section 12A, 80G tax deduction certifications, and MCA CSR-1 registration are currently undergoing official documentation and compliance verification processes. We do not make misleading claims of tax exemptions or statutory clearance without officially certified government documentation.

Corporates wishing to execute social responsibility programs can partner via direct project expense sponsorship, vendor payments, in-kind distribution of goods, or collaborative MoUs.
