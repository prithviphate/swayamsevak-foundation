What to Expect

  • Every Sunday Food Drives:

    Our teams gather on Sunday mornings in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar to package and distribute fresh food.

  • Winter Blanket Night Drives:

    During cold months, volunteers participate in night distribution of blankets to homeless people sleeping on streets.

  • Orphanage & Elderly Visits:

    Spend quality time celebrating festivals, bringing gifts, sweets, and sharing happiness.

  • Zero Commercial Fee:

    Volunteering is 100% voluntary and selfless. We value dedication and compassion above all.
